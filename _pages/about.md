# About Me

I am Ilona Marchetti, Associate Professor at the Vellmar Institute of Computational Science (VICS) and I lead the Adaptive Retrieval Lab (ARL).

## Academic background

I hold a Ph.D. in Computational Linguistics (2014) from Aldervik University and an M.Sc. in Data Science (2010) from the Nordhavn Institute of Technology.

## Research experience

Before joining VICS I was a Senior Researcher at the Nordic Institute for Language Data (NILD), Oslo (2018–2020). In 2016–2017 I was a visiting researcher at the Marenthal University Institute of Applied Linguistics. I teach Retrieval Systems in the MSc Data Science Programme at VICS.

## Research focus and interests

My research focus is cross-lingual dense retrieval and evaluation of retrieval-augmented generation.

- sparse-dense hybrid retrieval
- multilingual benchmark design
- citation-grounded generation
- low-resource NLP
